# Contributing to ThermaScalp

Thank you for your interest. This is an early concept, so the most valuable contributions right now are ideas,
critique and evidence.

## Ways to help

- **Clinical and scientific input:** literature on effective scalp temperatures, safety limits and protocols.
- **Thermal and fluid engineering:** heat load estimates, CFD, channel design review.
- **Mechanical design:** printable helmet CAD, manifolds, cart layout.
- **Electronics and firmware:** controller schematics, fail-safe design, PID tuning.
- **Patient and caregiver perspective:** what the real session routine looks like and what would make it easier.
- **Translations and documentation.**

## Ground rules

1. **Safety first.** Do not describe or promote use of this design on people. Changes that touch limits, fail-safes
   or clinical claims need a cited source or a measurement.
2. **Evidence over opinion.** Back numbers with references or data, and mark assumptions as assumptions.
3. **English in the repository:** code, comments, documentation and issues.
4. **Be respectful.** This project is about people going through a hard time. Keep discussion kind and constructive.

## Workflow

1. Open an issue describing the idea or problem.
2. Fork the repository and create a branch.
3. Make focused changes, updating the relevant documentation.
4. Open a pull request that explains what changed and why.
