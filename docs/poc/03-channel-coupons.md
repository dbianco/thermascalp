# PoC 3: Channel coupon rig

**Hypothesis:** a meshed channel network gives more uniform surface temperature than a serpentine at similar pressure drop.

**Build**
- Flat coupons (about 100 x 100 mm) in silicone or printed TPU: serpentine, mesh, and a mesh with varied pitch.
- Heated plate (constant flux) under a thin silicone skin layer.
- Pump, flow meter, two pressure taps, inlet/outlet thermistors.
- IR camera or a grid of thermocouples on the surface.

**Measure:** pressure drop vs. flow; surface temperature map; inlet-to-outlet delta; leak test at 2x working pressure.

**Pass:** meshed surface temperature spread is lower than serpentine at equal flow, and pressure drop stays within
the chosen pump's flow curve. Record print settings so results are reproducible.

Cost target: material and sensors in the low tens of euros per coupon set.
