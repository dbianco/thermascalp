# Open questions

The concept rests on assumptions that have not been tested. This list is the main source of work for the first
milestones. Contributions of data, literature and measurements are especially welcome.

## Clinical and physiological

- What scalp contact temperature and spread are actually effective, and what is the safe lower limit? (Concept
  assumption: about 15 °C, band 14 to 16 °C.)
- How does the effective setpoint vary with the chemotherapy regimen, hair type and head size?
- What are the right pre-cooling and post-cooling durations for the target regimens?
- How should comfort (headache, cold pain) feed into setpoint adjustment?

## Thermal

- What is the real heat load from a head and scalp, and how large are the heat gains from hoses and the room?
- Is 10 to 15 L of ice water enough for 4 to 6 hours without refilling? How does it vary with room temperature?
- Is it better to circulate water, or a water and glycol mix? What are the implications for biocompatibility and spills?
- Can supply-water temperature at the helmet be kept above a safe minimum without throttling the pump into poor
  circulation?

## Hydraulic and mechanical

- How much pressure drop do the meshed channels and baffles add, and can a small 12 V pump deliver enough flow?
- What is the best way to bond silicone channels to TPU in a leak-free way that survives a session?
- How can one helmet design fit a range of head shapes, with good contact and no pressure points?
- Which dry-break coupling is small, cheap and reliable enough?

## Electronics and software

- Sensor choice: DS18B20 or NTC/PT100, and the achievable accuracy and response time in situ.
- What independent hardware backstop is simplest and most robust?
- How should sessions be logged, and should data stay local only?
- Should an optional battery path be part of the first prototype or deferred?

## Hygiene and logistics

- How is the fluid path cleaned between patients or sessions? Is a disposable liner practical?
- What is the process for loading ice at the clinic, and does it suit nursing workflows?
- Is the 30 kg limit realistic with 15 L of water and ice plus the frame?
